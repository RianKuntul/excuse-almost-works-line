One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week: the
boiler's leaking all over the kitchen floor, the emergency plumber's on
the way, and nobody can leave the house today. Boring, specific, and
just plausible enough that a tired manager would almost believe it.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the excuse to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to
`/output/entry.txt`), after self-validating the gate: exactly one line,
1–140 characters, at most 20 words.
